Delete Your Vendor Account

Request deletion of your Foodzoid Vendor account and data.

Last updated: October 13, 2026

This page explains how vendors (restaurants and stores) can request deletion of their Foodzoid Vendor account and associated data.

How to Request Account Deletion

To delete your Foodzoid Vendor account, please contact us using either of the options below:

Email: support@foodzoid.in

Phone: +91 8707530475

What Happens Next

Once we receive your request, we will delete your account and associated data within 30 days.

What Data Is Deleted

Your vendor profile and the personal information linked to your account are removed. Some records may be retained where required to comply with legal, tax, or accounting obligations, after which they are deleted.
